New reviews and updates wait here, oldest first, until you approve them. Names of people are already shown as *** (Claude Haiku finds them); flags are things to look at. The writer gets a message either way.

Addresses picked from the app's address search come with coordinates: check the pin, then add it. An address only shows where the place is, not that the business is real, so you still decide that. For addresses typed by hand (and older suggestions) you need the coordinates: open Google Maps, press and hold the pin (right-click on a computer) and copy the two numbers, like 40.9389, -73.9952. The person who suggested it gets a message either way.

Search synonyms

When someone searches the word on the left, places matching any word on the right show up too. Parts of words work: "veterinar" finds veterinary and veterinarian.

Masked words

These are saved as their first letter and *** in reviews, names, about lines, collection names and suggestions. The list holds slurs; curse words aren't masked (you approve every review anyway). Only whole words count, so a word inside a longer word is safe unless you tick "Also longer words". Changes apply to new posts.

Photos

New photos wait in the Reviews tab with their review (up to 10 per review or update). Tick "Leave out" on any you don't want, then approve; declining a review removes all its photos.

Photos by review

One card per review, newest first (approved and waiting). Click a card to see its photos. Deleting can't be undone; the writer gets a message with your reason.

Leftover files

Files no photo uses any more: left out or declined photos that weren't deleted right away, deleted reviews and accounts, and uploads that didn't finish. Only files older than an hour are listed.
