The scale

Five ways to be treated.

Every review starts with one question: how did they treat you? The answer is the Gracio score.

  1. 01

    Rude

    Dismissive or disrespectful. You won't be back.

  2. 02

    Cold

    Got what you came for, and no one cared.

  3. 03

    Fine

    Nothing wrong, nothing memorable.

  4. 04

    Polite

    Friendly, attentive, glad you came.

  5. 05

    Gracious

    Went out of their way. You felt looked after.

How it works

Three steps, from visit to score.

  1. 01

    Visit

    Eat, shop, get a haircut or a repair. Anywhere in New Jersey or Manhattan.

  2. 02

    Rate how you were treated

    One tap from Rude to Gracious, then the details if you like. Check in at the business to mark it Verified.

  3. 03

    See what it's like now

    Switch between all time, the last 12 months and the last 30 days, and see who's improving or slipping.

Why Gracio

Built for the part after “how was the food?”

Star ratings blend the cooking, the prices and the parking into one number. Gracio keeps how you were treated front and center, and gives every detail its own score.

  • ◆

    Treatment first

    The score is how people were treated. Details like food or wait time are scored on their own.

  • ✓

    Verified visits

    Reviews checked in at the business, or with a receipt, are marked Verified.

  • ◷

    What it's like now

    Recent reviews show if a place is getting better or worse, not just how it was years ago.

  • ◎

    A person checks every review

    Names are hidden automatically and every review is checked before it's posted.
