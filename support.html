Questions

What is the Gracio score?

How a place treats people, from 1 to 5 bow ties, averaged from reviews: 1 Rude, 2 Cold, 3 Fine, 4 Polite, 5 Gracious. Details like food or wait time are scored on their own too, and count for up to 20% of a review's score when the reviewer rates them.

Can I change my review?

Yes, once every 30 days. Rate the place again and your update shows under your original review, so people can see how things changed. Only your latest rating counts toward the score.

What does Verified mean?

The review was written within 1,000 feet of the business, going by your phone's location. You can post from anywhere; reviews written away from the business just don't get the Verified mark.

What is a Trusted reviewer?

Someone with at least 5 Verified reviews or updates at 3 or more places in the last 12 months, an account at least 30 days old, and a good record. Their reviews count 1.25 times as much.

A business is missing or its details are wrong

Tap "Can't find the place? Add it as a business" in the app to suggest it, or report the listing from its page. You can also email us.

How do I report a review?

Open the review and tap Report. We look at every report.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. It removes your profile, reviews, collections and settings. You can also email privacy@gracioapp.com from the address you signed up with.

Is Gracio available where I live?

Gracio is starting in Bergen County, New Jersey and Manhattan, New York. More areas are coming.
