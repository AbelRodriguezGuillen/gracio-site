The short version. Review places you've actually been to, honestly. Be decent: no fake reviews, harassment, hate or spam. We may remove content and accounts that break these rules. Gracio has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

Using Gracio

  • You must be at least 13 years old.
  • One account per person. Keep your sign-in to yourself; you're responsible for what's posted from your account.
  • By using Gracio you agree to these terms and to our privacy policy.

Your profile

Your name, "about" line and profile picture follow the same rules as reviews. Use your own name or a nickname, not someone else's or an official-sounding one. Your profile picture must be you, or something you made or have the right to use: no photos of other people without their permission, no logos you don't own, and nothing offensive. Gracio staff check every new picture before others can see it, and we may decline a picture or reset a name, "about" line or picture that breaks these terms.

Your reviews

You own what you post. By posting, you give Gracio permission to show it in the app and on our website, and to use it to calculate scores. You can edit your review once every 30 days by posting an update, and you can delete your account (and your reviews) at any time.

Review rules

  • First-hand and honest. Only review places you've been to, based on your own experience.
  • No conflicts of interest. Don't review your own business, your employer, or a competitor.
  • No paid or traded reviews. Don't post reviews in exchange for money, discounts or freebies, and businesses must not offer them.
  • Be decent. No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content or graphic violence.
  • Respect privacy. Don't post anyone's personal information. Shout-outs to staff should use first names only.
  • No spam. No ads, links, or the same review posted again and again.

Reporting and moderation

Anyone can report a review or a business listing from the app. We review reports and may hide content, remove business listings, or suspend or close accounts that break these rules, with or without notice. If you think we got something wrong, write to support@gracioapp.com.

Businesses

Business listings come from public data (the Overture Maps Foundation), from users' suggestions and from business owners. Businesses can't pay to change their score or hide reviews. To fix a listing, report it in the app or email support@gracioapp.com.

Business accounts

If you claim a business on gracioapp.com/business, these extra rules apply:

  • Claim only a business you run. You must be its owner or a manager allowed to speak for it, and the details in your claim must be true. We may call the business to confirm, and we may decline a claim or remove your access at any time, including if you leave the business.
  • Keep your account secure. Business accounts must use two-step sign-in. You're responsible for everything done with your business access.
  • Replies. Replies to reviews follow the review rules above. Be respectful and stay on topic: no threats, no pressure on a reviewer to change or remove a review, no offers of money, discounts or freebies in exchange for a review or a change, and no one's personal information. A moderator checks every reply before it's shown, and people's names, phone numbers, email addresses and links are hidden as ***. Replies are shown as from the business, not from you.
  • Business details. The phone number, website, description and hours you submit must be accurate and yours to share. A moderator checks every change. By submitting them, you give Gracio permission to show them in the app and on our website.
  • No control over scores or reviews. A business account can't remove, hide or edit reviews, and it can't change a score. If you think a review breaks our rules, report it like anyone else.
